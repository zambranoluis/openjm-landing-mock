The essentials before you get started

Learn how the platform works, what each plan includes, and how you can get started.

What is OpenJM?

OpenJM is an artificial intelligence platform created in Jamaica for researching, writing, organising information, analysing files, and developing ideas through natural conversations.

Can I get started for free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes 100,000 monthly tokens, standard AI models, basic document analysis, and up to 20 active conversations.

What can I attach to a conversation?

You can work with images, documents, and code files. Supported formats, maximum file sizes, and specific limits will be indicated within the platform.

What is the difference between Free and Paid?

Paid offers greater capacity, advanced models, unlimited conversations and history, advanced document analysis, research with sources, image generation and analysis, more connections, and priority support.

How do additional credits work?

Credits allow you to extend your usage when you exceed the limits included in your plan. An active Paid plan is required to access additional capacity and advanced features.

Where is OpenJM available?

OpenJM is available worldwide and supports interactions in multiple languages, allowing individuals and organisations to use the platform across different countries and contexts.

How are my data and files managed?

The handling of conversations, files, and personal data will be explained in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What type of support will I receive?

The Free plan includes access to the Help Centre. Paid plan users receive priority support.
